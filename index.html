Save as MP4

Record your primary display to H.264 MP4 files, saved locally to your chosen folder.

Your recordings, your PC

No sign-up, cloud uploads or subscription. Your recordings stay on your computer.

Simple quality controls

Choose Quality 720p or Original resolution, plus 15, 30 or 60 FPS.

Quick shortcut

Start and stop recording using Ctrl + Shift + F9, even while you're in another app.
